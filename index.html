THE BASICS

What exactly
does Article 17 say?

“Everyone has the right to own property, alone as well as in association with others.”

— Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 17

Article 17 recognizes a person's right to own property and protects them from being unfairly deprived of it. Property may include a home, land, personal belongings or a business.

OWNHave property as an individual.
SHAREOwn property together with others.

DID YOU KNOW?

Article 17 protects
individual and shared ownership.

That means the idea of ownership is not limited to one person. Property can also be owned together with other people.
