UNIVERSAL DECLARATION OF HUMAN RIGHTS

The Right to
Own Property.

Everyone has the right to own property, alone or together with others, and should not be unfairly deprived of it.

Explore Article 17 ↓
01 / UNDERSTANDING IT

What is
Article 17?

Article 17 protects a person's right to have and keep property. This can include a home, land, personal belongings or a business.

ARTICLE 17

“Everyone has the right to own property, alone as well as in association with others.”

Universal Declaration of Human Rights
02 / WHY IT MATTERS

Property is more
than things.

01

Security

A home and personal belongings can provide stability and a sense of safety.

02

Independence

Owning property can help people make decisions about their lives and futures.

03

Opportunity

Property such as land or a business can support education, work and economic opportunities.

03 / WHEN RIGHTS ARE VIOLATED

What can
go wrong?

When property rights are ignored, people may lose homes, land or belongings unfairly. This can increase insecurity and inequality.

  • Unfair removal from a home or land
  • Property taken without a fair process
  • Discrimination in access to property
  • People being denied equal protection of their belongings
