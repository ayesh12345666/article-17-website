UNIVERSAL DECLARATION OF HUMAN RIGHTS

The Right to Own Property.

Everyone has the right to own property, alone or together with others, and should not be unfairly deprived of it.

01 / THE BASICS

What is
Article 17?

Article 17 is part of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. It protects the right to own property and the right not to be unfairly deprived of it.

ARTICLE 17

“Everyone has the right to own property, alone as well as in association with others.”

Universal Declaration of Human Rights

In simple words: people should be able to own property and should not lose it unfairly.

04 / WHEN RIGHTS ARE VIOLATED

What can
go wrong?

Property rights matter because unfair treatment can affect people's security and opportunities.

01

Unfair removal

Being forced from a home or land without fair protection.

02

Confiscation

Property being taken without a fair legal process.

03

Discrimination

Unequal treatment when accessing or protecting property.

04

Weak protection

Systems that make it difficult to defend legitimate ownership.
