UNIVERSAL DECLARATION OF HUMAN RIGHTS

The Right to
Own Property.

Article 17 protects the right to own property, alone or together with others, and the right not to be unfairly deprived of it.

01 / UNDERSTANDING IT

What is
Article 17?

Article 17 is part of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. It protects a person's ability to own and keep property. Property can include a home, land, personal belongings or a business.

ARTICLE 17

“Everyone has the right to own property, alone as well as in association with others.”

Universal Declaration of Human Rights
In simple words: people should be able to own property and should not lose it unfairly.

03 / WHEN RIGHTS ARE VIOLATED

What can
go wrong?

A property right becomes meaningful when people are protected from unfair loss, discrimination and abuse.

  • Forced displacement
    People may be unfairly removed from homes or land.
  • Unfair confiscation
    Property may be taken without proper legal process.
  • Discrimination
    People may face unequal treatment when accessing or protecting property.
  • Weak protection
    Without fair institutions, ownership disputes can leave people vulnerable.
